E Commerce Conceptual Data Model

Entities

Customer
Customer places orders and owns a cart.

Cart
Cart contains products selected by the customer.

Product
Product represents an item available for purchase.

Order
Order represents a customer's completed purchase.


Relationships

Customer 1:M Order
One customer can place many orders.

Customer 1:1 Cart
One customer has one cart.

Cart M:N Product
A cart can contain many products and a product can appear in many carts.

Order M:N Product
An order can contain many products and a product can appear in many orders.
